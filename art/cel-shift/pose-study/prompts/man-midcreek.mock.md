Character action-pose sheet for a video game. Not a scene, not a user
interface, and not pixel art.

STRICT REFERENCE ADHERENCE — INPUT IMAGE 1 is the RPG sprite sheet. Use input
image 1 only for the six body poses, silhouettes, facing directions, gesture
arcs, ordering, and spacing. INPUT IMAGE 2 is the canonical Cel Shift male
technician sheet. Use input image 2 for the character's identity, proportions,
clothing, palette, and rendering style. Do not copy the RPG character,
costume, sword, shield, or pixel-art treatment.

LAYOUT — exactly six separate full-body figures in one evenly spaced horizontal
row, left to right, matching the top row of the pose reference:

1. Neutral ready stance, weight balanced, right-hand tool held low, left arm
   carrying equipment close to the body.
2. Wide planted stance making a powerful horizontal two-handed sweep.
3. Wide stance with the left hand extended outward to trigger a diagnostic
   effect.
4. Crouched braced stance behind a protective object as sparks strike it.
5. Full running stride toward the right, both feet clearly separated.
6. Upright victory or signal pose with one tool raised high overhead.

MIDCREEK TRANSLATION — preserve those poses but replace every fantasy object
with credible data-centre work equipment. Use a rugged handheld diagnostic
scanner or insulated service tool in the ready pose; a long torque wrench or
breaker bar in the sweeping pose; a coiled service cable and restrained teal
diagnostic pulse in the casting pose; a removable perforated rack door or
equipment access panel in the blocking pose; no carried object while running;
and an insulated service tool raised overhead in the final pose. Effects are
small, graphic indicators of cable motion, signal flow, electrical contact, or
diagnostic status. No magic, leaves, runes, fantasy weapons, heraldry, or
combat armor.

THE CHARACTER — the canonical Cel Shift man technician, the same recognizable
person in all six poses. Adult proportions, roughly six-and-a-half heads tall,
average build, 1.73 m, clean-shaven with no beard, moustache, or stubble.
Short dark hair under a blue hard hat with ear defenders. Lime yellow-green
hi-vis vest with orange trim and broad silver retroreflective bands over a
long-sleeved slate work shirt. Blue straight-cut denim jeans, brown work boots,
tool belt, and corded orange ear plugs. Keep the hard hat, face, body
proportions, clothing, and palette consistent in every figure.

STYLE — Cel Shift. Clean cel-shaded production art with large flat colour
areas, at most one hard-edged cool shadow tone, and thick confident
uniform-weight dark outlines around silhouettes and major internal forms.
Clear adult anatomy and strong readable silhouettes. Plain warm off-white
studio background with no scenery, no grid, and no labels. Small flat ground
ellipses are allowed.

Palette: background #F0EDE4, hi-vis lime #C8D94A, orange trim #E8763A,
retroreflective silver #D6DBE0, slate shirt #55707F, denim #4A6485, boots
#3A3128, hard hat #2C6FB8, teal diagnostic accent #2FB8A8, warning yellow
#FFC93C, ink outline #23282D.

CONSTRAINTS — exactly six figures and no extras. No overlapping figures. No
lettering, numerals, words, logos, badges, watermark, signature, decorative
frame, or interface. No photorealism, gradients, ambient occlusion, texture,
grain, glow bloom, depth of field, motion blur, cute proportions, chibi style,
fantasy costume, sword, shield, spell circle, foliage, or flames.
